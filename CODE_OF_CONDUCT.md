# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want meta-ads-guard to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, experience level, nationality,
gender identity, religion or any other personal characteristic. Contributors and maintainers pledge to make taking
part in this project a harassment-free experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the project.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions before drawing conclusions.
- Keep discussions focused on the issue or pull request at hand.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's credentials, customer data or personal details.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, such as trolling or spamming issues.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any other channel
where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and
may temporarily or permanently block contributors who repeatedly or seriously break it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the e-mail address on the
[@saizul GitHub profile](https://github.com/saizul), or open a private report through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/saizul/meta-ads-guard/security/advisories/new) if no other channel suits.
Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not shared without consent.
